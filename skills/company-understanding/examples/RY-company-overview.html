④ Customers & competitors

  • Customers: ~17M+ retail and business clients (B2C + B2B), plus wealth clients and institutional/corporate clients in Capital Markets. No single-customer concentration risk — it's a mass-market bank.
  • TD Bank — the other giant; bigger US retail footprint (and its US regulatory baggage).
  • BMO — deeper US commercial push via Bank of the West.
  • Scotiabank — more tilted to Latin America; RBC is more Canada + US-affluent.

⑥ Management & ownership

  • CEO Dave McKay — leading RBC since 2014; a career RBC banker.
  • No founder involvement (RBC dates to 1864); widely held with no controlling shareholder.
  • Institutions dominate the register; insider ownership is immaterial for a bank this size.

⑦ Shareholder returns

  • Dividend: C$6.04/share for FY2025, up +7.9%; the quarterly payout was raised to C$1.64 (+6%). A long, steady record of increases.
  • Buybacks: RBC runs a normal-course issuer bid, but the exact FY2025 repurchase dollar amount — needs verification.

⑧ How this bank dies → three risks

The death scenario: a deep Canadian housing bust sends mortgage and consumer losses through the roof at the same time the economy stalls — the loan book RBC has spent 160 years building becomes the thing that sinks it.

  • Canadian housing / consumer credit: RBC is heavily levered to Canadian mortgages; a price crash or unemployment spike hits provisions hard.
  • Rate cycle: net interest margins and loan demand swing with central-bank rates — cuts can squeeze the spread that is the core engine.
  • Integration & regulation: digesting HSBC Canada and operating across tightening capital rules leaves less room for error.
